## What Refueler is

Refueler brings a new kind of customer to your counter: privacy-minded, Bitcoin-native,
and already on their way. We are not a delivery app and we are not a loyalty scheme.
We are a quiet ordering rail that sits alongside the till you already use — never
in front of it.

This guide gets you started – and stays useful.                              [CHANGED: final line 20]

---

## What the terminal does

Three things, and it does them without fuss:

- **Shows your order queue** — every order, its items, and where it stands:
  preparing, ready, collected.
- **Lets you add a walk-in order** — anyone at the counter, in a few taps.
- **Reads the trains** — live arrivals on the Fenchurch St line, so your team
  can time the counter to the platform.

Everything runs in the browser on the tablet you already own. Nothing to install.

**Tip:** bookmark refueler.io/merchant/ on your tablet's home screen — the terminal
stays open in the background and is always one tap away.                      [NEW: bookmark/PWA tip]

## Signing in

Signing in happens once, by email — no password to remember or lose.

1. On the tablet, open **refueler.io/merchant/**.
2. Enter the owner's email address and tap **Send Sign-in Link**.
3. Open the email on the same tablet and tap the link. The subject line is
   *"Your sign-in link — Refueler."*
4. The terminal opens. You are in.

The sign-in link is for the **owner** only. Day to day, your staff use a PIN
(below) — no email access needed for a normal shift.

**If the owner is away:** the terminal stays signed in for an extended period.
Your Refueler account manager will agree the right session length with you at
onboarding and review it at every visit. If the session does expire while you're
away, any staff member can contact support at support@refueler.io and we'll get
the terminal reopened within the hour.                                [CHANGED: tightened + support path]

## Your two PINs

The terminal uses two short codes. Keep them apart.

- **Staff PIN** — begins a shift. Any member of staff enters it on the lock screen
  to open the order queue and start working.
  *(Your staff PIN is in the details box at the foot of this guide.)*

- **Owner PIN** — unlocks the **Owner** view: your takings, and your controls.
  *(Your owner PIN is on the tear strip. Keep it somewhere safe — not on the counter.)*

To change a PIN, contact support and we'll reset it. Self-service PIN management
is on the way.

---

## Opening your venue

When you arrive, tell the terminal you're open. Tap **OPS** on the pill at the
top right of the screen.

Find **Accepting orders** under *Venue Status*. Switch it **on** to open —
you're now taking orders. Switch it **off** to close — the counter is shut and
no new orders arrive.

There is a second control, **Pause new orders**, for the busy moment when the
queue is full but you're not shutting for the day. Paused, existing orders carry
on; nothing new joins the queue until you switch it back.

---

## End of day

When the last of the day is done:

1. Switch **Accepting orders** to **off** in the **OPS** view.
2. **Sign out** (see below).
3. Put the tablet on charge overnight — the terminal needs power to be ready
   for the morning.

**Power and internet:** the terminal runs in the browser and needs a live internet
connection. If the power goes out or the connection drops, the terminal will be
unavailable until it's restored. Fall back to your normal till and contact support
— we'll confirm everything is intact once you're back online.

---

## Taking a walk-in order

For anyone standing at the counter who isn't ordering on the app:

1. Tap **New Order**.
2. Enter an **identifier** — a table number, or "Counter." It's how your team
   knows whose order it is.
3. Enter the **item(s)**.
4. Add a **note** if you need one. Otherwise, leave it blank.
5. Confirm. The order drops straight into your queue.

No fixed menu to navigate — you write what was ordered. It suits a kitchen
that changes with the day.

**Menu shortcut — coming soon:** your full menu, pre-loaded into the terminal so
staff can pick items rather than type them. Your account manager will set this
up with you when it's ready.

## The arrivals strip

The dark band across the top is your line to the platform. It shows the next
trains into **Fenchurch St** and the minutes until each one lands. When a wave
is two minutes out, you'll want the counter ready.

If the feed goes quiet, the strip says so and simply waits — nothing breaks,
and the rest of the terminal carries on.

---

## The three views, at a glance

Tap between them on the pill at the top right.

- **QUEUE** — the working view. Orders, walk-ins, arrivals. Where your shift lives.
- **OPS** — open and close, pause the queue, sign out.
- **OWNER** — takings and controls, behind the owner PIN.

*(PAPER / CARBON beside the pill switches the screen between light and dark.
Your choice — the terminal remembers it.)*

---

## Signing out

When you're done, sign out — don't just close the tab. A signed-out terminal
is a locked door.

You'll find **Sign Out** in both the **Queue** view and the **OPS** view, so
whoever locks up can reach it without hunting. It returns the terminal to the
sign-in screen, ready for the next shift's PIN.

If you signed out by mistake, enter the staff PIN on the lock screen to resume.
